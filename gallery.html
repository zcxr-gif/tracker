Submission rules

Every photo is screened by staff on Discord before it appears in the gallery. Submissions that break these rules are rejected.

  • Hide the UI. No menus, widgets or overlays — the plane, not your battery indicator.
  • Keep it natural. Original, unedited photos only: no filters or colour grading.
  • The whole picture. Don't crop — submit the full frame.
  • Centre the aircraft.
  • Good lighting. No backlit shots; the side you're showing should be lit.
  • Spotter-style shots. Mid-air shots are likely to be rejected.
  • Tell us what it is. Aircraft type and livery, e.g. British Airways Airbus A380.
Required
  • Taken with Infinite Flight's built-in HD screenshot in replay mode.
  • Taken in the beta app — 25.1 renders are no longer accepted.
  • Cropped, edited or UI-obstructed photos will not be accepted.
