FLIGHT WINDOW + MAP UPDATE — NOTES
Pull request #235 (branch: claude/flight-window-taxi-cleanup)
==================================================================


1. FLIGHT INFO WINDOW — CLEANED UP (Legacy + Horizon)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Nothing is shown twice any more. Each piece of data lives in one place:

  - Header / route bar: airports, times, total distance, phase, ETE.
  - Top glance row (Horizon) / PFD (Legacy): live altitude, ground speed,
    vertical speed, heading.
  - Pilot status: just the status. No more "Active" + "Pilot is active".
  - This flight: flown / remaining distance, time since departure, total trip
    time, planned cruise (only if the flight plan has one), average and max
    ground speed, max altitude.
  - Airports: the taxi card and the arrival-airport card, together.
  - Position & weather (was "Navigation", which clashed with the Navigation /
    Flight Plan switch): latitude, longitude, true airspeed, wind, outside air
    temperature, next waypoint, nearest airport, "currently over".

Removed because they repeated something else:
  - The timer card's "Remaining" (same as ETE in the header).
  - The second progress bar and "Time airborne" in This flight.
  - The Flight Plan footer's total distance and estimated time.
  - Altitude, heading and ground speed in Navigation (already on the PFD and
    the top row).


2. FUEL AND PASSENGERS — REMOVED
------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Fuel and Cabin sections removed from every flight window (Legacy, Horizon,
    Simple and Card), plus the Passengers and Block fuel rows and their toggles.
  - fuelEstimator.js and cabinMap.js deleted.


3. HORIZON — GAP ABOVE THE PHOTO FIXED
------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Some photos (especially the 2nd/3rd photo in the carousel) sat a few pixels
    down from the top, leaving a strip of window colour above them. The photo
    now always starts at the very top, and re-fits when the width changes.


4. RECENT FLIGHTS IN SEARCH — FIXED
------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Tapping a recent flight now opens it even if the pilot respawned or you
    switched servers (it finds the same pilot under the same callsign) — the
    same thing searching the callsign again used to do by hand.
  - If a landed flight has no replay, you now get a message instead of the
    search just closing.


5. NEW: TAXI CARD (flight window, under "Airports")
------------------------------------------------------------------
Shows how the flight taxied out, and after landing, how it taxied in.

Map views (switch above the map):
  - Satellite (default) — satellite imagery under the drawing.
  - Map — plain diagram. Satellite/Map choice is remembered.
  - 3D — tilted satellite view with terrain and buildings, only when tapped.
  - None of these use Mapbox map loads (Esri imagery, free terrain tiles,
    OpenFreeMap buildings, MapLibre for 3D).
  - Tap the map to enlarge it.

On the drawing:
  - The taxi path follows the taxiway centre lines, with rounded, realistic
    turns. The takeoff roll, landing roll and pushback are drawn as flown
    (never snapped).
  - Pushback shown as a white dashed "tail" from the gate.
  - Every taxiway is lettered like an airport chart: taxiways used = black on
    yellow, the rest = yellow on black.
  - A tick mark across the path for every minute on the move.
  - Orange marker where it stopped, red where it held short, with how long.
  - "Push", "Taxi" and "T/O" time tags.

Checklist under the map (each step ticked off, in order), e.g.:
  ✓ Pushed back · 17:05 Z · 45 m
  ✓ Started taxiing · 17:08 Z
  ✓ A · 3m 01s · 0.6 NM
  ✓ B · 3m · 0.6 NM
  ■ Stopped on B · 2m
  ✓ K · 2m 59s · 0.5 NM
  ■ Held short of 04L · 1m 18s
  ✓ Took off from 04L · 17:16 Z
  (after landing: "Landed on 04R · time" … "Parked · time")
  While still taxiing, the current taxiway is marked live.

Totals: runway, taxi time, total time stopped, distance.
Out / In tabs when both exist. Tap the airport code to open the airport.

Notes:
  - Taxiway names/lines come from OpenStreetMap; they load when the card
    scrolls into view. Airports with no taxiway letters in OSM show no letters.
  - Pushback and short stops are only as accurate as Infinite Flight's position
    reports; a very quick one between two reports won't show.


6. MAIN MAP — THE SAME, ON THE FLOWN PATH
------------------------------------------------------------------
  - The flown trail's taxi-out and taxi-in follow the taxiways (same logic as
    the taxi card), for the open flight and pinned flights. Pushback, rolls and
    the flight itself are untouched; the line still ends at the plane.
  - Costs nothing extra while airborne (only recalculated on the ground).
  - Path info along the flown path, as small fixed-size callouts (they never
    grow with their text): a short code on top, the time/duration below, and a
    little tail pointing at the dot.
      In flight, any zoom: T/O, TOC FL360, CLB/DES FL370 (each step to a new
      level), TOD, LDG — each with its time.
      On the ground, zoomed in: pushback tail, STOP / HOLD with durations,
      PUSH / TAXI / PARK times, taxiway letters (small yellow squares).
  - "Path info" button in the flight window's photo button row turns all of
    this off/on (the trail itself stays). Remembered.
      Phone: icon button next to replay / camera — reachable in peek.
      Desktop: labelled "Path info" pill with a green on-light.
    (Horizon and Legacy windows; Simple/Card don't have the button yet.)


7. PFD / ND CUT OFF OR DISAPPEARING — FIXED
------------------------------------------------------------------
  - The PFD and ND had CSS colour filters. On iPhone Safari, a filtered element
    inside the scrolling window only paints what was on screen when it was
    drawn, so after scrolling the top/bottom went missing or the display
    vanished. Filters removed (colours are set directly instead).
  - The ND was also sized slightly taller than its square screen, so its bottom
    was always cut off by a few pixels. It now fits exactly.
