How to play
Tap an aircraft (or its strip) for its commands, laid out as in the Infinite Flight ATC client: a numbered menu, with the pilot's request in green and the likely next call as a tile. The log shows only that aircraft's radio while it's selected.
Drag an airborne target to vector it: let go and pick an altitude from the ladder (its present one is green). Drag onto a fix for direct, onto a final for the approach, onto its runway to clear it to land. On the ground, drag to a runway, taxiway or gate.
Hold a target for the quick menu: altitude, hold, reminder, colour, tag placement.
Arrivals check in asking for an approach. Answer (expect, continue as filed, vectors), get them down and onto the final at 30° or less, outside the gate and below the glideslope, then hand them to Tower.
Departures push, taxi, hold short. Line them up or clear them for takeoff, then climb them out through their exit.
Separation 3 miles or 1,000 feet, more behind heavies and supers. 250 knots below 10,000 feet.
ATIS from the frequency list: runways, approaches, remarks, NOTAMs, then publish.

The rulebook Real-life rules follow FAA JO 7110.65; Infinite Flight rules follow the IFATC manual. Breaking one costs points and the log names the rule.
