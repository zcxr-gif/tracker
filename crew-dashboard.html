    Notifications

    Notify your Discord

    Paste one Discord webhook and everything the crew center does — applications, flight reports, route changes — posts to that channel. That is all most VAs need.

    In Discord: Server Settings → Integrations → Webhooks → New Webhook → Copy URL.

    Send some of it somewhere else

    Recruitment

    New applications, and accept / decline decisions.

    Flight reports

    Filed, approved and rejected — one feed — plus pilot promotions.

    Route network

    Routes added, edited, removed or imported.

    Events

    Published, changed and cancelled. Signups don’t post — a busy event would fire forty of them in an evening.

    Email applicants

    Bring your own email provider so decision emails come from your domain and your account. If you don’t set one up, no emails are sent — applicants just use their status page.

    Staff roles

    Name a role and tick exactly what it can do. The tick boxes appear inside each role once you add one — there is no separate permissions screen. Roles belong to this crew center only; another VA’s roles are its own.

    Only you, the owner — and anyone you give “Create staff roles & assign the team” to — can see or change this. Owners always have full access. A staff member with no role assigned keeps the everyday permissions until you give them one, but never the owner-level ones below. Nobody can grant a permission they don’t hold themselves, so handing someone this page cannot make them the owner.

    Start from a job

    Adds a role you can rename and re-tick. Nothing about it stays “a preset” afterwards.

    Assign staff

    Match a staff member’s login username to a role. Anyone not listed here keeps full access.

    Your data · Supabase

    Your crew data lives in your Supabase project, not ours — roster, hours, flight reports, applications, applicant emails and your pilots’ logins. All Inflight stores is your own staff logins and your crew center’s branding. Leave the platform and you keep everything, because we never held it.

    Set it up for me

    Paste a Supabase access token and we’ll do the rest — create or pick a project, install the tables, and connect it. Takes about a minute.

    1. Sign in at supabase.com (free plan is fine).
    2. Open Account → Access tokens and generate one.
    3. Paste it here.

    This token can manage your whole Supabase account, so by default we use it for this setup only — never logged, never leaving this request.

    Which project?

    Pick the region closest to most of your pilots.

    Your database needs an update

    The same kind of access token you used to set this up. We run the current setup script against the project you're already connected to — it only ever adds, so your crew, flights and logins are untouched, and your keys don't change.

    Rather do it by hand?

    Open “I’ll do it myself” below, copy the setup SQL and run it in your project’s SQL editor. It’s the same script and it’s safe to re-run — that is the upgrade.

    Updates to your database

    Make one at Account → Access tokens. We check it opens your project before we keep it, store it encrypted, and use it only to add new tables and columns to this project.

                    Running out of room?
                    
    
                        
    Flight reports are almost always the biggest thing, and they grow forever. Old ones you have already credited can be exported to CSV from the flight log and removed from the database — the hours stay on the roster, because credited hours live on the pilot, not on the report.
    
                        
    Event artwork and pilot photos count against a separate Storage allowance rather than the database. If files are what is full, they are in your Supabase project under Storage.
    
                        
    Upgrading the plan is Supabase’s side, not ours. Your project is yours — the plan, the bill and the limit are between you and Supabase, and nothing here changes when you move up.
    
                        
    The ceiling shown above assumes Supabase’s free plan. We can’t see which plan your project is on, so if you have upgraded, treat the bar as a rough guide and trust your Supabase dashboard.
